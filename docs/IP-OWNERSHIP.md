### 3.1 Definition

"Pre-existing IP" refers to all software, source code, documentation, architecture, design, and other materials in this repository that existed **prior to** and **independently of** any academic, internship, or organizational use.

### 3.2 Characteristics of Pre-existing IP

The pre-existing IP in this repository includes:
- Original source code developed by the original authors

- Business logic and implementation

- Development history and decision records

### 3.3 Evidence

## 5. Ownership

### 5.1 Retention of Rights

The copyright holder(s) retain all intellectual property rights in the pre-existing IP, including but not limited to:
- Copyright rights
- Ownership rights
- Rights to license
- Rights to modify
- Rights to distribute

### 5.2 No Assignment

This repository and its documentation do not constitute an assignment of intellectual property rights to any user, organization, or entity.

### 5.3 Independent Development

The software was developed independently by the original authors without external direction, funding, or requirements from any organization with which academic/internship users may be associated.

## 6. Academic/Internship Use

### 6.1 Limited Permission

The copyright holder may grant limited permission to use the software for academic research or internship purposes ("Authorized Use").

### 6.2 Authorized Use Characteristics

Such permission is:
- Non-exclusive
- Non-transferable
- Non-sublicensable
- Limited to specific purposes
- May be revoked

### 6.3 What Authorized Use Permits

Authorized Use may permit:
- Running the software for intended academic/internship purposes
- Reading the source code for learning purposes
- Making modifications for authorized research (if explicitly permitted)

### 6.4 What Authorized Use Does NOT Permit

Authorized Use does NOT permit:
- Redistribution of the software
- Commercial use
- Public deployment
- Claiming ownership or authorship
- Creating derivative works for redistribution
- Using the software as the basis for commercial products

---

## 7. Host Organization / Company

### 7.2 No Rights Through Use

The host organization or company where an internship is conducted does NOT acquire any intellectual property rights in the software merely because:
- The software is used during the internship
- The intern has access to the source code
- The host organization provides facilities or resources

### 7.3 Separate Agreement Required

Any transfer of intellectual property rights requires a separate written agreement signed by all relevant parties, including the copyright holder(s).

## 8. Modifications

### 8.1 Pre-existing IP Modifications

Modifications to the pre-existing IP by anyone other than the original authors remain the intellectual property of:
- The original authors (for pre-existing components)
- The modifier (for their contributions)

### 8.2 Future Ownership

Ownership of modifications made by authorized users depends on:
- The specific terms agreed upon
- Applicable law
- Whether the modifications constitute independent or joint authorship

### 8.3 Clarification

This document does NOT claim that all future modifications belong to the original authors. Ownership of modifications depends on who created them and under what terms.
